# Applied AI & Machine Learning for Business Decisions

Hands-on projects applying machine learning, deep learning, natural language
processing, and generative AI to real business problems. Each project is framed
around the decision it supports, the trade-offs considered, and a clear,
actionable recommendation.

Completed as part of the **Postgraduate Program in Artificial Intelligence &
Machine Learning: Business Applications**, The University of Texas at Austin,
McCombs School of Business in 2025.

## Capabilities demonstrated

- **Data analysis & statistics:** exploratory analysis, hypothesis testing, feature engineering
- **Predictive modeling:** classification, ensemble methods, hyperparameter tuning
- **Model evaluation:** choosing metrics that reflect business costs and risks
- **Deep learning:** neural networks, convolutional networks, transfer learning
- **NLP & generative AI:** text processing, embeddings, sentiment analysis, LLMs, retrieval-augmented generation
